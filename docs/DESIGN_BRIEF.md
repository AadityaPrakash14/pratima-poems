# DESIGN BRIEF — Pratima साहित्य संग्रह

## Project Identity
A digital literary home for the Hindi literary works of Pratima. It should feel like opening a cherished book or diary: quiet, warm, personal, elegant, and timeless.

## Project Vision
**A home for timeless Hindi poetry and stories.**

The website preserves and presents Pratima's Hindi poetry and stories for today's readers and future generations.

## Audience
- **Primary:** People who love Hindi literature.
- **Secondary:** Friends and family.
- **Future:** Future generations discovering these writings years or decades from now.

## Brand
**Name:** प्रतिमा  
**Subtitle:** साहित्य संग्रह  
**Tagline:** शब्द जो वर्षों तक डायरी में रहे, अब एक नई यात्रा पर हैं।

## Design Philosophy
Reading should feel calm. The interface should support the literature rather than compete with it.

Desired qualities:
- Warm
- Calm
- Elegant
- Reflective
- Personal
- Literary
- Timeless
- Authentic

Avoid:
- SaaS/dashboard styling
- Generic blog styling
- Social-media-feed styling
- Loud gradients
- Excessive cards or animation
- Gamification

## Creative Direction
Visual inspiration:
- Old books
- Handwritten diaries
- Fountain-pen ink
- Handmade/aged paper
- Libraries
- Reading corners
- Warm evening light
- Hindi literary journals
- Bookmarks
- Subtle botanical/ink illustrations

## Language
All literary content is in Hindi using Devanagari. The interface should be Hindi-first.

Primary navigation:
- मुखपृष्ठ
- कविताएँ
- कहानियाँ
- लेखिका

Preferred literary UI copy:
- **सराहना करें** instead of Like
- **अपनी अनुभूति लिखें** instead of Comment
- **साहित्य पढ़ें** instead of Start Reading

## Homepage
The homepage begins with the author and literary identity, not a content grid.

Sequence:
1. Hero
2. Author introduction
3. Featured poems
4. Featured stories
5. Literary quote
6. Footer

Hero direction:
```text
प्रतिमा
साहित्य संग्रह

शब्द जो वर्षों तक डायरी में रहे,
अब एक नई यात्रा पर हैं।

[ साहित्य पढ़ें ]
```

## Reading Experience
The reading page is the heart of the product.

Priorities:
- Comfortable reading width
- Large readable Devanagari typography
- Generous line height and spacing
- Minimal distractions
- Clear title and author attribution
- Subtle metadata
- Previous/next navigation
- Accessible engagement controls

## Authenticity
Where handwritten originals are available, eventually support:
**मूल हस्तलिखित पृष्ठ देखें**

This should present a scan/photo of the original manuscript and reinforce the archival nature of the site.

## Guiding Principle
> **Does this feel like a digital home for timeless Hindi literature?**

If a design decision does not support that feeling, reconsider it.
